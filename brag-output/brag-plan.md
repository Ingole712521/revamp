# Brag Plan: Nehal Ingole

## What is this app?
A personal portfolio for Nehal Ingole, a software developer in Pune who ships React and TypeScript products and the AWS, Docker, and CI/CD path that keeps them in production — including AnimioUI, Revio, and an EMQX cluster scaled to 300K concurrent connections.

## The angle
A quiet product film of the portfolio itself. The page is the product: a black field with a white dot grid, the name at full scale, then the site's own sentence, then three real project cards. No hire-me language. The work speaks.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black `#000000` with the site's 32px white dot grid. "Nehal Ingole" locks in at display size. Under it, the role types out: "Software Developer", caret blinking, the way the hero does. A small emerald status dot sits with the name, the same online mark as the avatar.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The site's headline holds, full and readable: "I build modern web apps, reusable UI systems, and the cloud workflows that keep them in production."
- Three project cards from the projects section arrive one by one and then hold together: AnimioUI, Revio, EMQX on AWS. Each card shows the real name and a short impact line already on the site.

## Outro / punchline
The domain, alone: `nehalingole.in`. Under it, the stack the site actually names: React, TypeScript, AWS. Hold. Fade the music.

## User flow worth showing
The product is the portfolio page. The reading path is the flow: land on the name → read the claim → see the work. No separate app sits behind the page. Centerpiece scenes recreate the hero lockup and the project cards, not a diagram of the résumé.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film of a developer portfolio
- Interpretation: four scenes, mixed-case Inter, soft crossfades, one idea per scene, no joke, no list dump. Motion is fast in, then a long hold.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.5 seconds

- Display font: Inter
- Body font: Inter. Code wallpaper, if used as a faint ground, is Geist Mono.
- Strongest visual element: the dotted field plus the hero name lockup, then the bordered project cards (rounded, zinc hairline, name + impact).

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM). Cue preset: `.claude/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`
- Music treatment: start at 0, sit under the picture, fade out across the last 1.2s of the outro. Do not duck for voice (narration is off).
- Music cue guidance: strong cues in the window at 1.60s, 5.80s, 12.65s, 17.91s. Lock the name settle near 1.60s, the headline settle near 5.80s, and the domain near 17.91s. Card arrivals snap to every other beat around 11.06, 12.65, and 14.22 so each label can be read. Ignore a cue if it cuts a hold short.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Let RMS lift a soft halo behind the name and a little presence on the cards. No waveform, no equalizer, no strobe.
- SFX posture: sparse and soft. One quiet landing on the name. Light key ticks only while the role types. One soft UI placement per project card. Nothing percussive on the outro.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed role in scene 1; three card arrivals in scene 3; domain landing in scene 4.
- Restraint rule: polished tone. No glitch, no punch impacts, no stacked SFX. Music stays a bed, not a drop.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Name — 5.0s
Black dotted field. "Nehal Ingole" in Inter, large, tracking tight, white. A 10px emerald dot sits at the baseline. "Pune, India" in zinc-400 under the name. Starting just after the name is readable, "Software Developer" types in with a caret, then holds. The full name is on screen for the whole scene (well past a 0.8s label floor). The typed role finishes with at least 1.2s settled.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the role types character by character. The name and place are already there.
Audio intent: the bed opens clean. A soft land when the name arrives. Quiet key ticks only during the type.
Audio-coupled idea: type "Software Developer" with sparse key ticks.
Music: warm bed, vol-11. Name settle beat-locked near 1.60s.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The line — 6.0s
Same field. One sentence, two or three lines, mixed case, large enough to read and small enough to sit with air around it:

"I build modern web apps, reusable UI systems, and the cloud workflows that keep them in production."

Sixteen words. Entrance under 0.5s, then hold the full sentence for at least 4.8s before it leaves. Nothing else competes with it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: the bed carries the sentence. No SFX under the words.
Audio-coupled idea: headline settle near the 5.80s strong cue (0.8s into the scene).
Music: same bed, no swell that fights the type.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The work — 5.5s
Three project cards in a row, recreated from the projects section: rounded rect, dark card fill, white hairline, name, one impact line, a small tag.

1. AnimioUI — "Creator of AnimioUI" — tag Next.js
2. Revio — "Reviews PRs in seconds" — tag TypeScript
3. EMQX on AWS — "300K concurrent connections" — tag Terraform

Cards arrive one by one, then the full set holds. Each name is 1–3 words, so each card stays fully visible at least 0.8s before the next arrives, and the trio holds together for the rest of the scene (about 2.5s). Do not put the long project descriptions on screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards, in that order.
Audio intent: a soft placement sound with each card. Bed stays even.
Audio-coupled idea: card sequence on every other beat near 11.06s, 12.65s, 14.22s, then hold.
Music: same bed. The 12.65s cue can catch the second card.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Domain — 4.0s
Dot grid thins. Centered: `nehalingole.in`. Under it, smaller, zinc-400: "React · TypeScript · AWS". Both lines hold at least 2s settled. Music fades out across the last 1.2s. No extra tagline invented for the close.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: one quiet land on the domain, then the bed leaves.
Audio-coupled idea: domain settle near 17.91s.
Music: fade out. No final sting over the fade.
Transition mood: soft fade to black at the end.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat but held back — a warm business bed under a still picture.
**Audio summary:** One bed from the first frame, a few soft UI sounds on the name, the typing, and the three cards, then a fade under the domain.

Scene durations: 5.0 + 6.0 + 5.5 + 4.0 = 20.5s.
