# Nehal Ingole — brag

## Style Prompt
Quiet product film of the portfolio's own dark theme. A black field, a 32px white dot grid, and Inter at display size. One emerald dot. Motion arrives fast and then holds. Nothing neon, nothing gradient-filled, nothing that the site itself does not already do.

## Colors
- Background: `#000000` — dark theme canvas
- Text: `#fafafa` — dark foreground
- Muted: `#a1a1aa` — zinc-400 labels
- Accent: `#10b981` — emerald status dot
- Card: `#1c1c1f` — dark card, hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`

## Typography
- Display and body: Inter. This is the portfolio's font (`--font-inter`), used on purpose.
- Mono: Geist Mono for tags, the domain, and the caret. The site's `--font-geist-mono`.
- Name around 128px, semibold, tracking near `-0.04em`. Headline around 64px. Body and labels 22px or larger.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not invent claims, emails, phone numbers, or SaaS filler.
- Do not use a waveform, equalizer, or particle field.
- Do not restyle the project cards into a different product.
- Do not swap Inter for a "more interesting" display face.
- Do not flash type. Fast in, then hold.
